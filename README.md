# 7ambot: A Personalized Companion Robot

7ambot is a custom companion robot project I am currently building from scratch in Algeria.

While many creators and engineers abroad build highly personalized robots, I wanted to be the first Algerian student to introduce and document this concept locally. I am designing this robot to be as unique and personalized as possible, integrating my knowledge of CAD, electronics, and microcontrollers.

Rather than building in isolation, I am documenting the entire journey through a signature video [series online](https://www.instagram.com/the_you7mb?rpxt=Z3l3OGY3bmo5b2gx&utm_source=qr). I am sharing every step of the progression—both the successes and the failures—so people can watch, provide constructive criticism, and get inspired to build their own projects.

Alongside 7ambot, I will also be working with [sesame robot](https://github.com/dorianborian/sesame-robot), a robot created by the engineer @dorianborian. I will be using Sesame as my "fâr tajârib" (experimental subject) to learn, test new ideas, and gain hands-on robotics experience that I can apply directly to the 7ambot build.
